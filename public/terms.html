Wayfarer terms of use

Wayfarer is a private travel planning tool for you and invited travelers. Do not invite people to another person’s trip without authorization, submit unlawful content, or attempt to bypass membership controls.

Plans and financial records are entered by travelers. Verify booking details, dates, local time zones, transport schedules and venue hours with the relevant provider. Suggestions and route estimates do not create reservations, guarantee availability or replace navigation and local safety information.

Google Maps features are also subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Maps, place search and route estimates require network access and service configuration. No offline Google tiles are provided.

Expense splits and settlement suggestions are accounting records only. No payment processing is provided. Confirm the recipient and amount outside this app before making a payment.

Local demo mode stores everything in the current browser and does not authenticate or collaborate with other accounts. Connected mode requires a configured service provider. Keep backups; clearing browser storage can remove unsynchronized edits.

See the privacy policy for data use and deletion information.
