Your trips are private.

Wayfarer stores itinerary, expense, repayment, packing and queued edit data in your browser’s IndexedDB. Connected mode stores shared records in the configured Supabase project. Only authenticated trip members can read shared records; personal packing items belong to their account.

Authentication uses Supabase email links. Invitations are bound to the recipient email and expire after seven days. The app does not send invitation emails; owners share the generated link themselves.

Google receives search terms, place IDs, coordinates and route requests when you use live map features. Google content stays in memory; the app stores linked place IDs and your own planning data. See the Google Privacy Policy.

If configured and explicitly used, AI receives destination, dates, interests, budget, available time, and existing activity titles. Private notes, reservation references, financial item details and traveler email addresses are not included in the AI request. Exchange-rate providers receive currency codes.

Clearing browser storage can destroy unsynchronized changes. Revoking access prevents future server requests but cannot erase copies already downloaded by someone else. Device security and browser profiles remain important. Export your expenses before clearing storage.

Contact your trip owner or the operator of your Supabase project to request removal of hosted data. This private installation does not sell trip data or operate a public marketplace.
